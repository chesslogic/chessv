# Target a family-preserving 10x10 augmentation budget

Type: grilling  
Status: resolved

## Question

What material shape and family policy should constrain the 10x10 CPU augmentation?

## Answer

Aim for roughly twenty pawn units of added material:

- four minor-tier pieces, described conceptually as about 3.5 pawn units each;
- two Tower/Gardener-tier pieces, described conceptually as about 2 pawn units each;
- two additional pawns.

Use Towers rather than Scouts for the 10x10 concept. Preserve Enemy Army identity by replacing forward orthodox-role pieces with corresponding pieces from the selected family at roughly similar material. Do not require every family to field literal Bishop, Knight, or Tower pieces.

This is a target shape, not an exact arithmetic result. Catalog values expose a deliberate mismatch to resolve later: Tower is 325 rather than about 200, while Gardener is 250. The exact family matrix and calibrated total remain open.
